# Africa-Building – Construction & Development

**Africa-Building** builds houses and buildings, sells real estate and rents homes across Africa. One team supports every step of your property project, from the first plan to the final key.

## Our services
- **Construction:** houses, villas, apartment blocks and commercial buildings
- **Real estate sales:** plots, houses and buildings with clear paperwork
- **House rental:** homes for families, professionals and companies
- **Design and plans:** floor plans, sections and facades
- **Renovation and management:** extensions, repairs and rental property management

## Our approach
1. Understand your need and budget
2. Visit the site or property and prepare a clear offer
3. Sign the agreement, then build, transfer or prepare the property
4. Inspect together and hand over the keys
